Monkey D. Luffy

Captain of the Straw Hat Pirates and the man who dreams of becoming the Pirate King.

Who is Luffy?

Monkey D. Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates. He is adventurous, fearless, and always protects his friends. His biggest dream is to find the One Piece and become the Pirate King.

Luffy's Abilities

Gum-Gum Powers

Luffy's body has rubber-like properties, allowing him to stretch his arms and legs.

Haki

Luffy can use different types of Haki to sense enemies, strengthen attacks, and overpower opponents.

Gear 5

Gear 5 is Luffy's powerful transformation that gives him incredible freedom and strength.

What Luffy Stands For

For Luffy, being the Pirate King means being the freest person on the sea. He values friendship, freedom, adventure, and never giving up on his dreams.
